## Not errata

- **Pyright diagnostics in frozen code** are recorded in `docs/environment_and_code_quality.md` section 5. They come from library type inference, and spot checks found no defect. A type diagnostic is not evidence of a calculation error.
- **Mixed line endings in frozen files** are preserved byte for byte by `.gitattributes`; they are not defects.
