| 2026-11-20 17:45 | **Revision F21-1, authorized by the owner.** The final audit (`audit/scientific-audit.md` section 21, finding F21-1) found that the origin-local strategy differed from the fixed strategy only at the six origins 1976–1981, where 10–15 no-change errors existed at h = 15.
